Functional packaging

First, it has to run.

A packing machine picks up a flat carton, opens it, fills it and closes it. It does this again and again, very fast. If one carton opens late or is not square, the whole line stops. We make cartons that do not stop your line.

Running: Reverse tuck (RTI)

750cartons a minute. That is how fast our cartons run on customers' packing lines, day in and day out, without a stop. And they do it on lower-GSM recycled duplex board.

What decides it

Five things decide the run.

We check many things on a carton made for a packing machine. These five matter most, so we check them on every job. The line below shows what goes wrong, and then our carton.

  1. FlatnessThe carton stays flat, so the machine picks one at a time.
  2. Surface slipSmooth enough to feed. Not so smooth that the stack slides.
  3. CreaseEvery carton opens with the same force.
  4. Glue seamThe glued side holds when the carton is opened fast.
  5. SquarenessThe carton opens square, not twisted.
Decorative packaging

Then it has to be seen.

A carton is made in seven steps, from a plain sheet to a glued carton. We print six colours and a coating in one run on our KBA and Heidelberg presses. Every step has to be in exact register with the one before.

  • Blank sheetWe check the board before printing
  • Six-colour printA four-colour picture and two Pantone colours
  • CoatingDone on the same press, in the same run
  • Hot foilHot foil stamped on the print
  • EmbossA raised design you can feel
  • Die-cutting and creasingCut and creased in one go on Bobst die-cutters, to your reverse tuck shape
  • Folder-gluerGlued and folded flat on Bobst folder-gluers, ready for your line
What we make

Every carton has its own needs.

A fluted carton, a foil carton and a pharma carton each need different things. We know what each one needs, and we set up the job for it. We make almost every carton shape, and custom designs when you need one.

  • Mono cartonsOne layer of board. The most common retail carton
  • Fluted cartonsPrinted board laminated on fine flute. Strong, with good print
  • Liner cartonsAn extra layer inside to make it stiffer
  • Window cartonsA cut-out window with clear film
  • Foil and Metpet cartonsGold or silver foil, or shiny metallised board
  • Blister and backer cardsCoated to seal onto a plastic blister. For hanging packs
  • Pharma cartonsWith Braille, and checked by camera
